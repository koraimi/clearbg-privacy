# Privacy Policy for ClearBG

Last updated: November 9, 2026

ClearBG ("the app") removes the background from your photos.

## Your photos
Photos you choose are processed **entirely on your device**. The app does not upload your photos to any server, and we do not collect, store or see them. Edited pictures are saved only to your own phone when you tap Save.

## Permissions
The app asks to read photos you pick and to save the finished picture to your Gallery or Documents folder. It needs internet access only for showing ads (when ads are enabled).

## Advertising
This version of the app does not show ads. Current or future versions may show ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show ads and measure them. You can control ad personalization in your phone's Google settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Accounts and personal data
There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address.
